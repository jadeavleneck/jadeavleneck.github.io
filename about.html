About

My work is driven by a curiosity about people, organizations, and the systems that shape our experiences.

I am particularly interested in organizational sustainability, employee well-being, meaningful work, and the conditions that allow individuals and communities to thrive.

Much of my recent study and writing has explored how organizations can pursue long-term success while supporting the humans who make that success possible.

This site serves as a home for ongoing projects, reflections, and future research directions.
