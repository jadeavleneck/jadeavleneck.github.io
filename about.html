About

Jade Avleneck, MBA, SHRM-CP

Exploring workplace well-being, systemic burnout, and the systems that shape people’s experiences at work.

I am interested in what happens when purpose, commitment, and human limits collide. As an HR professional and aspiring researcher, I study workplace well-being, systemic burnout, organizational culture, and the systems that influence how people experience work.

Through research and writing, I examine the relationship between purpose, burnout, recovery, and the conditions that make work sustainable over time.
