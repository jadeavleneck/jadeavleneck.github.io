About

Jade Avleneck, MBA, SHRM-CP

Exploring workplace well-being, systemic burnout, and the systems that shape people’s experiences at work.

I am an HR professional and aspiring researcher with a particular interest in how people experience work. My work explores workforce sustainability, employee well-being, burnout, workplace culture, and the factors that help people remain engaged and healthy throughout their careers.

Through research and writing, I examine the challenges that shape working life, from organizational practices and leadership decisions to individual experiences of stress, purpose, growth, and recovery. My goal is to better understand what makes work sustainable for the people who do it.

About The Lucidity Codex

The Lucidity Codex is the home for my research, writing, frameworks, and scholarly projects. It began as a way to organize ideas and knowledge gained through MBA study and has continued to evolve alongside my academic and professional interests.

Part research archive and part working notebook, the Codex serves as a place to document ongoing learning, explore questions, and develop ideas related to workforce sustainability and the human side of work.
