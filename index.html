The Lucidity Codex

Research, writing, and ongoing inquiry into workplace well-being, burnout, organizational culture, and the realities of meaningful work.

The Lucidity Codex is an evolving collection of research, questions, and developing ideas focused on understanding how people sustain meaningful work without losing themselves in the process.
